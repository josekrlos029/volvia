Volvia Biz 1.2 puts the whole counter on your phone:
• Preview before stamping: number of stamps, purchase amount and rewards waiting.

• Customers: search, profiles with history, and sign-up at the counter with a QR and WhatsApp sharing.
• For owners and admins: statistics, messages, campaigns, team and kiosk mode.
• Pick the phone's location and show the sign-up QR full screen.
